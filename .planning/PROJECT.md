# Project Vision: Modo Colab 🤝

## Vision
Transform underutilized capacity into high-value specialized services for local businesses. Modo Colab is a barter economy platform connecting local establishments (cafes, restaurants, bakeries, etc.) with local talents (offering any specialized skill, such as design, photography, consulting, maintenance, music, IT, etc.) who seek real-world experience, portfolio building, and local perks (consumables/credits).

## Core Loop
1. **Business Posts Mission**: Define what is needed (e.g., "5 product photos").
2. **Talent Applies**: Business reviews portfolios and selects the best fit.
3. **Value Exchange**: Talent delivers work, Business approves, Consumption Credits are released.

## Current Context
The project is in a stabilization phase, refining the core user journey (MVP). A codebase map has been established, identifying a Service-Repository pattern using FastAPI and SQLAlchemy.

## Milestone: MVP Stabilization & Launch
**Objective**: Finalize the core journey from mission creation to credit redemption, ensuring a polished experience for the first cohort of users.

### Must-Haves
1. **Full Mission Lifecycle**: From posting to approval and credit release.
2. **Consumption Wallet**: Functional credit system for talents to redeem products and experiences at participating establishments.
3. **Public Portfolio**: A professional presentation layer for talents to showcase their work and ratings.
